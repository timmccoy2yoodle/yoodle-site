Yoodle Education Services x FEC KC

Let's keep the conversation going about YES (Yoodle Education Services) our new agency program that creates opportunity for the local community to invest in their own future!

Special guests James Bryant, Director of Career Services, and Rohina Behrmann, Director of Employer Services, of The Full Employment Council join us as we discuss their roles in business and industry in Missouri.

Together they create and implement training programs that are employer centered, to support industry needs, as well as providing support to the willing and eager workforce in Missouri. Their goal is to get individuals "skilled up" so that they can succeed on the job.

Hosted by Shawn Ortega
