Questions.

What is a fractional CMO?

A fractional CMO is a senior marketing leader who works inside your organization part time on a fixed contract. You get the strategy, the plan and the management of the work without a full-time executive salary.

How long is an engagement?

Six months. The first 90 days are a sprint to diagnose, plan and launch. The following months run and improve the system.

Who hires a fractional CMO?

Organizations with a marketing staff and no senior marketing leader: growing companies, multi-location businesses, nonprofits and public-sector teams. We lead the team you have and build it up in house.

How many clients does Tim take on?

Only a few at a time. Two openings are available now.
