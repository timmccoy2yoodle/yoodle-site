Questions.

What is fractional marketing leadership?

A senior marketing leader works inside your organization part time on a fixed contract. You get the strategy, the plan and the management of the work without a full-time executive salary.

How long is an engagement?

Six months. The first 90 days are a sprint to diagnose, plan and launch. The following months run and improve the system.

Who is it for?

Organizations with real revenue or a real mandate and no senior marketing lead: growing companies, multi-location businesses, nonprofits and public-sector teams between hires.
