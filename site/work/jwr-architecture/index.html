The project.

Joe Rathermel is a Kansas City based architect who needed a top-to-bottom branding system developed for his practice, JWR Architecture. Joe’s work and design sense lives at the intersection of contemporary and historic; drawing inspiration from both modern design, and a diligent attention to detail.

The work.

The results

The results find balance in the harmonious blend of form and function. The brand resides outside of any specific historic context, but still fits beautifully in a contemporary setting. These definitive characteristics were pulled from Joe’s personal taste in architecture and design. The brand communicates a sense of sophistication and professionalism; two important prerequisites for entrusting someone to design your living and/or working space.
