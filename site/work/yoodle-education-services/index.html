The project.

Yoodle Education Services (YES) is the workforce school Yoodle built. With the UMKC School of Computing and Engineering as university partner and the Full Employment Council of Kansas City as workforce partner, Yoodle's people taught 16-week courses that give working adults debt-free training for technology jobs. Classes began in early 2021. A new AI digital marketing course was written in 2025.

What we made.

  • Web developer fundamentals: HTML, CSS, JavaScript and WordPress, with a nonprofit website built as the class project
  • Digital marketing: design thinking, brand strategy, content, SEO, social media and analytics
  • Full-stack courses in MongoDB, Express, Angular and Node, and in ASP.NET Core
  • A four-week IT support course
  • Curriculum written and taught by Yoodle's designers, developers and strategists
  • The Let's Skill Up brand, info sessions, flyers and advertising that filled each cohort
  • Enrollment and funding through the Full Employment Council and KansasWorks
