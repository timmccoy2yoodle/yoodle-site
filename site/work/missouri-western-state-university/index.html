The project.

Missouri Western State University chose Yoodle through a public RFP for market research and brand development. The work grew into a rebrand, a marketing campaign and advertising. Yoodle designed the new MO West logo.

CONFIRM: results to cite and the final brand strategy deliverable

Visit missouriwestern.edu

What we made.

  • Brand perception research: internal, community and prospective-student studies
  • Brand strategy
  • Communication audit and creative concept
  • The MO West logo: a primary mark, a stacked mark and secondary MW and MO West marks, approved by the university in November 2024
  • "More at MO West" campaign creative for outdoor and social
  • Campaign and marketing plan
  • Brand launch video, 2025
  • Ongoing marketing support on retainer, 2024 to 2025
