The project.

Río Hondo College brought Yoodle® in for one engagement with nine goals: measure the website, test it with real users, set the rules for running it, then write, build and film the content that fills it.

Nine goals.Fully delivered.

  1. 01

    Analytics and goals.

    Goal setting and Google Analytics, so the college agreed on what the website is for before changing it.

  2. 02

    Looker dashboards.

    Dashboards the web team reads without an analyst, with a user guide and a plain-language glossary.

  3. 03

    Usability testing.

    Card sorting, tree testing and a first-reaction survey, turned into navigation and content recommendations.

  4. 04

    Governance.

    A web content governance guide and RACI chart, delivered with a recorded consultation.

  5. 05

    Content journeys.

    Model content journeys for the pages that matter most to students choosing a program.

  6. 06

    Writing for the web workshop.

    A recorded workshop and guide, so college staff can keep writing pages to the same standard.

  7. 07

    Page writing, accessibility and migration.

    Pages written and optimized, ADA compliance testing, and content migrated from the client's final list. Pages completed May 13, 2024. Migration completed July 26, 2024.

  8. 08

    Media for the web.

    Video series, a campus tour, a brand video and photography, shot for use on the pages we built.

  9. 09

    Support programs finder.

    A filter and sort tool for student support programs, built and deployed into the college's WordPress codebase.
