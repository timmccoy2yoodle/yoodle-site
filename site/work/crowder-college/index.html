The project.

Crowder College is a community college in southwest Missouri with a plain promise on its homepage: changing lives and strengthening communities, one student at a time. Yoodle designed and built the new crowder.edu on WordPress, and it launched in 2026.

Discovery with stakeholder and user interviews and an online survey, a new sitemap and navigation, wireframes and two creative concepts, six page templates coded to Section 508 and tested for ADA compliance, a WordPress build, migration of the top 100 pages, analytics, SEO guidance, editor training with a written manual, launch and post-launch support.
