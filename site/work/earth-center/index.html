The project.

The EARTh Center is the Environmental and Natural Resources Technology Center, a National Science Foundation Advanced Technological Education center that supports environmental technology programs at colleges.

Yoodle won the public RFP run by Central Carolina Community College in 2024 and kicked off that April. Website maintenance agreements followed in December 2024 and June 2025.

Visit earthcntr.org

On the site.

  • Immersive Ed Center with instructional videos and simulations
  • Job Task Analysis Tool
  • Media library and Environmental Tech Map
  • DACUM examples and a best practice guide for programs
  • Events and newsletter signup
