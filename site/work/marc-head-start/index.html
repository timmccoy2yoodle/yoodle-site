The project.

MARC Head Start, a program of the Mid-America Regional Council, serves more than 2,400 infants, toddlers, preschoolers and their families in Clay, Jackson and Platte counties.

A website design and build for the Mid-America Regional Council's Head Start program.

Visit marcheadstart.org

  • Provider locator

  • Family success stories

The story.

The challenge

Reimagine a brand that has national reach but low equity locally. This was the challenge we took on working with an integrated program and operations team with MARC - Mid-America Regional Council.

The process

"One of the biggest responsibilities of management is to look after the organizations’ DNA."

We worked through our CX Finder™ methodology and developed a new brand system as well as user friendly web interface focused on creating the best experiences for parents and caregiver partners.
