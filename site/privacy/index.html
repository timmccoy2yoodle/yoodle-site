Privacy Policy.

Effective November 27, 2026

Who we are

Yoodle LLC (“Yoodle®,” “we,” “us”) is a brand, technology and marketing agency in Kansas City, Missouri. This policy explains what information we collect through yoodle.com, the free accessibility scan and calls booked through the site, and what we do with it.

Information we collect

  • What you send us. Your name, work email, organization, role, website address and anything you write in a form or an email to us.
  • Scan requests. The website address you ask us to scan, the date, and a record that you accepted our Terms, including which version.
  • Scan results. What our software observes on the public pages of the website you asked us to scan: page addresses, page code, the platform the site runs on and the accessibility problems found. We don't go looking for personal information on those pages, but public pages sometimes contain names or contact details, and small excerpts of page code can appear in a report.
  • Bookings. When you book a call, Google Calendar collects your name, email and chosen time and shares them with us.
  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages people read. It sets cookies and records information such as your approximate location, your device and browser, and the pages you view. It doesn't tell us who you are.
  • Technical logs. Our hosting providers record standard server information such as IP address, time and the page requested, for security and troubleshooting.

How we use it

  • To run the scan you asked for, prepare your report and send it to you.
  • To reply to you, schedule and hold calls, and prepare estimates and remediation plans.
  • To follow up about the report or service you asked about. You can tell us to stop at any time.
  • To keep records of requests and of agreement to our Terms.
  • To protect the site and the scan from misuse, and to meet legal obligations.
  • To improve our services, using aggregated or de-identified results that don't name you or your website.

How we share it

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for other companies’ advertising. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who work for us: Netlify (website hosting and form submissions), Google (analytics, calendar scheduling and email), the hosting provider that runs our scanner, and our email delivery provider.
  • AI software. We use AI software from Anthropic to help analyze scan results and draft reports and plans. A person at Yoodle® reviews every report before it's sent.
  • Contractors who work on Yoodle® projects under confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities or other parties when the law requires it, to protect our rights or others’ safety, or as part of a sale or reorganization of our business.

How long we keep it

We keep form submissions, scan results and reports for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, and longer if you become a client or if the law requires it. Records of agreement to our Terms may be kept for as long as needed to show that agreement. You can ask us to delete your information sooner.

Your choices

  • See, correct or delete. Ask what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, through the contact form. We respond within 30 days.
  • Stop follow-up. Tell us and we'll stop contacting you about a report or service.
  • Analytics. Block Google Analytics with your browser settings or with Google’s opt-out add-on. The site works without it.
  • State privacy rights. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state law, such as the right to know, delete, correct or appeal a decision. We honor those requests where the law applies, and we don't treat you differently for making one.

Security

We limit access to your information to people who need it, and reports are reachable only through a private link. No method of storing or sending information is completely secure, and we can't guarantee security.

Children

This site is for organizations and the adults who work for them. It isn't directed to children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Where information is processed

We and our service providers process information in the United States. If you use the site from another country, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the current version. If a change is significant, we'll say so on this page.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: use the contact form. Yoodle LLC, Kansas City, Missouri.
