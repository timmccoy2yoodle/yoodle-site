Websites, applications and digital experiences designed around the people who use them, and built to work for every one of them.

The approach.

A college, court or agency website is a public service. People arrive with a task: apply, register, pay, file, find a person. We design around those tasks, build on the platform your team can maintain, and test every page for speed and accessibility before it goes live.

We're platform agnostic. Our live work runs on WordPress, Drupal, Modern Campus CMS and plain static HTML. We'll tell you plainly when your platform is the problem and when it's not.

Questions.

Which platforms does Yoodle build on?

WordPress, Drupal, Modern Campus CMS (OmniUpdate) and static sites. We work on the platform you already have, and recommend a change only when it will save you money or risk.

Do you make websites ADA compliant?

Yes. We audit against WCAG 2.1 Level AA, fix the issues in the site's own code and content, retest, and deliver a conformance report. We don't use overlays or widgets.

Can you work under a prime contractor?

Yes. We regularly deliver design, development and accessibility work as a teaming partner or subcontractor.
