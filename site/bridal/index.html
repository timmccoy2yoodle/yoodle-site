Bridal

Bridal marketing.Proven in five stores.

Savvy Bridal and Vow Ready run five stores in Kansas City, St. Louis and Fort Lauderdale. Tim McCoy is their fractional CMO, and Yoodle builds their tools and trains their team. It's the same model we bring to your shop.

Talk about your shop

A true story.

Savvy Bridal opened in 2013. It grew to three cities, then added Vow Ready, an off-the-rack brand, in 2026.

It didn't grow on an agency retainer alone, and it didn't grow on an in-house team alone. It took four parts working together, and that's the model we bring to other shops. Savvy Bridal is Yoodle's second fractional CMO client, after the American Jazz Museum.

Four parts.

  1. 01

    A marketing leader.

    A fractional CMO who owns the plan, the budget and the numbers, and answers to the owner.

  2. 02

    An agency behind them.

    Yoodle builds what the plan calls for: brand, websites, ads, content and reporting. No hiring wait.

  3. 03

    An in-house team.

    Your own people run the day to day: social, events, follow-up, the stores. We hire with you, train them and hand over the playbook.

  4. 04

    Partners, managed.

    BridalLive, I Do Society, Yelp, TikTok, Meta, Google: a bridal shop runs on platforms, and every one needs setting up, customizing and watching. We manage them all, and the people behind them, so you don't have to.

A full marketing team.Firing on all cylinders.

What a five-store bridal group looks like once the four parts are in place.

  1. 01

    Creative direction.

    Every campaign starts with one idea and one voice. Savvy Bridal sounds like a private appointment. Vow Ready sounds like a gown she takes home today. Nothing ships that blurs the two.

  2. 02

    A production team.

    An in-house crew shoots in the stores and publishes every day. We hired them with the owner, trained them, and built the system that moves work from idea to post without the owner chasing it. CONFIRM: team roles and publishing rhythm

  3. 03

    Launches.

    When the fifth store opened in St. Louis on November 2, 2026, the site, the listings, the ads and the content were ready. It booked 40 new brides in its first three weeks.

  4. 04

    Leadership.

    Tim answers to the CEO and sits down with the store managers every quarter, so the stores and the marketing team work from one plan.

Problems, solved.For her and for the team.

Each of these started as a problem on the shop floor. When no software fixed it, we built the tool.

Walk-ins had nowhere to wait.

Vow Ready takes walk-ins, and nobody wants to spend a Saturday standing in a bridal shop waiting for a turn.

So we built the Q. She joins the line from a kiosk or her phone, leaves for coffee, and gets a text when her stylist is ready. The store sees the whole line on one screen.

Five stores, and no single view of them.

Sales lived in one system, ads in several more, and every store had its own logins. The owners could not see the business in one place.

So we built the growth dashboard: sales, bookings and ad spend, store by store, on one screen the leadership team opens every week.

Questions.

Do you work with shops other than Savvy Bridal and Vow Ready?

Yes. The system was built with them so it could be installed in other shops.

What does a fractional CMO do for a bridal shop?

Leads marketing as a member of your team: sets the plan, manages the budget, the agency work and your outside partners, and builds and trains your in-house team so the shop isn't dependent on outside help.

What results has the model produced?

From January to September 2026, against the same months of 2025, revenue at the three Savvy Bridal stores rose 35% and gown orders rose 35%, while ad spend rose 7%.

Is Savvy Bridal your only fractional CMO client?

No. It's the second. The first was the American Jazz Museum in Kansas City, a twelve-month engagement in 2023 and 2024.

Will you work with a shop in my city?

Not within 60 miles of a Savvy Bridal or Vow Ready store, open or planned. Everywhere else, yes.

How long is an engagement?

Six months. The first 90 days install the system. The rest trains your team to run it.
