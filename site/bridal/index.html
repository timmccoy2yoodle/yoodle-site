Bridal marketing.Proven in five stores.

Savvy Bridal and Vow Ready run five stores in Kansas City, St. Louis and Fort Lauderdale. Tim McCoy is their fractional CMO, and Yoodle builds their tools and trains their team. It's the same model we bring to your shop.

A true story.

Savvy Bridal opened in 2013. It grew to three cities, then added Vow Ready, an off-the-rack brand, in 2026.

Tim McCoy took over its marketing as fractional CMO CONFIRM: month and year Tim took over and built the department. The fifth store, Vow Ready St. Louis, opened on November 2, 2026. It's Yoodle's second fractional CMO engagement, after the American Jazz Museum.

It didn't grow on an agency retainer alone, and it didn't grow on an in-house team alone. It took four parts working together, and that's the model we bring to other shops.

Four parts.

  1. 01

    A marketing leader.

    A fractional CMO who owns the plan, the budget and the numbers, and answers to the owner.

  2. 02

    An agency behind them.

    Yoodle builds what the plan calls for: brand, websites, ads, content and reporting. No hiring wait.

  3. 03

    An in-house team.

    Your own people run the day to day: social, events, follow-up, the stores. We hire with you, train them and hand over the playbook.

  4. 04

The job.At Savvy Bridal.

A fractional CMO comes in to lead. This is what that means in a five-store bridal group, week to week.

  1. 01

    Lead.

    Tim answers to the CEO. He runs the marketing meeting every Monday and a quarterly marketing and sales meeting with the store managers, so the stores and the marketing team work from one plan.

  2. 02

    Build the team.

    A brand manager, a marketing coordinator and a brand assistant in Fort Lauderdale now run the day to day: social, email, the blog, store events. We hired with the owner and trained them on the job. CONFIRM: team roles and when each was hired

  3. 03

    Manage the partners.

    The ad agency, the paid search manager, the app developers and the BridalLive account each have one person to answer to. Tim reads their numbers every week and decides what changes.

  4. 04

    Decide and delegate.

    Two brands, kept apart. Savvy Bridal is the private, guided appointment. Vow Ready is the off-the-rack gown she takes home that day. Every campaign, page and dollar belongs to one or the other. The CMO makes that call and the team carries it out.

  5. 05

    Build the systems.

    The team edits the website through its own editor, and changes are reviewed on a staging site. Leads from the site land in BridalLive and in the store's inbox. Stylist onboarding is written down, shift by shift, in the training platform.

  6. 06

    Measure and update.

    One dashboard, refreshed every Monday, shows revenue, gown orders, new brides, close rate and return on ad spend for each store. Tim reports on it to the team every week, and the plan changes when the numbers do.

Questions.

Do you work with shops other than Savvy Bridal and Vow Ready?

Yes. The system was built with them so it could be installed in other shops.

What does a fractional CMO do for a bridal shop?

Leads marketing as a member of your team: sets the plan, manages the budget, the agency work and your outside partners, makes the high-level calls, and builds and trains your in-house team so the shop isn't dependent on outside help.

What results has the model produced?

From January to September 2026, against the same months of 2025, revenue at the three Savvy Bridal stores rose 35% and gown orders rose 35%, while ad spend rose 7%. Ad spend per gown order fell 21%.

Is Savvy Bridal your only fractional CMO client?

No. It's the second. The first was the American Jazz Museum in Kansas City, a twelve-month engagement in 2023 and 2024.

Will you work with a shop in my city?

Not within 60 miles of a Savvy Bridal or Vow Ready store, open or planned. Everywhere else, yes.

How long is an engagement?

Six months. The first 90 days install the system. The rest trains your team to run it.
