Bridal

Bridal marketing.Proven in five stores.

Savvy Bridal and Vow Ready run five stores in Kansas City, St. Louis and Fort Lauderdale. Tim McCoy is their fractional CMO, and Yoodle builds their tools and trains their team. It's the same model we bring to your shop.

Talk about your shop

A true story.

Savvy Bridal opened in 2013. It grew to three cities, then added Vow Ready, an off-the-rack brand, in 2026.

It didn't grow on an agency retainer alone, and it didn't grow on an in-house team alone. It took four parts working together, and that's the model we bring to other shops.

Four parts.

  1. 01

    A marketing leader.

    A fractional CMO who owns the plan, the budget and the numbers, and answers to the owner.

  2. 02

    An agency behind them.

    Yoodle builds what the plan calls for: brand, websites, ads, content and reporting. No hiring wait.

  3. 03

    An in-house team.

    Your own people run the day to day: social, events, follow-up, the stores. We hire with you, train them and hand over the playbook.

  4. 04

    Partners, managed.

    BridalLive, I Do Society, Yelp, TikTok, Meta, Google: a bridal shop runs on platforms, and every one needs setting up, customizing and watching. We manage them all, and the people behind them, so you don't have to.
