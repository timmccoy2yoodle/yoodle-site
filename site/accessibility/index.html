Questions.

What is the ADA Title II website compliance deadline?

April 26, 2027 for state and local governments serving 50,000 people or more, and April 26, 2028 for smaller governments and special districts. The Department of Justice extended both dates by one year in April 2026. The standard is WCAG 2.1 Level AA.

Does the ADA web rule apply to colleges and universities?

Public colleges and universities are covered by ADA Title II and the WCAG 2.1 AA rule. Private institutions fall under ADA Title III and, if they take federal funds, Section 504.

Can an automated scan make my website compliant?

No. A scan finds a large share of issues, but not all. Conformance needs manual testing too. Our fix includes the manual checks and a retest.

Do you use an accessibility overlay or plugin widget?

No. We fix the site's own code, templates and content.

Which platforms do you work with?

Any. WordPress, Drupal, Modern Campus, static HTML and hosted builders all go through the same steps.

Sources: U.S. Department of Justice interim final rule, Federal Register, April 20, 2026. General information, not legal advice.
